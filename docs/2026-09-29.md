## [Open TTS server hits 10 req/s, sub-50ms latency on one H100](https://github.com/nari-labs/nari-qwen3-tts)

`discovery` · github

nari-qwen3-tts wraps the open Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-CustomVoice weights in a serving stack that handles 10 requests per second with sub-50ms p95 time-to-first-audio on a single H100. It ships three tunable profiles trading off latency against throughput, under Apache 2.0. Drop-in TTS serving without training or licensing a proprietary model. (2 minute read)

## [EAServe fixes GPU starvation in multimodal LLM serving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31551v1)

`discovery` · arxiv

Multimodal LLM serving splits into three stages, Encode, Prefill, and Decode, but existing systems treat Encode as a bolt-on service, leaving its GPUs underused while Prefill and Decode starve downstream. EAServe instead repositions Encode as a control point that regulates request flow into the rest of the pipeline. (2 minute read)

## [When local LLMs can replace frontier-model calls, quantified](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31540v1)

`discovery` · arxiv

Touchstone routes network-automation tasks to small local models (1-8B params) only when a cheap deterministic check can verify the output, escalating the rest to a frontier LLM. On conflict-detection and intent-translation it hits 98.6% and 93.8% accuracy while escalating just 16-17% of requests. Keep sensitive configs local whenever a task admits a cheap correctness check. (2 minute read)
